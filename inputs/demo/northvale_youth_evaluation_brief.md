# Evaluation Brief: Independent Evaluation of the Pathways Programme

Northvale Youth Trust

## About us and the programme

Northvale Youth Trust is a charity supporting young people aged 16 to 24 in the Northvale
district. Pathways is our three-year employment programme: it combines mentoring, paid work
placements with local employers, and help with job applications. Around 450 young people will
take part between 2025 and 2028. The programme is funded by the Fenwick Foundation, which asks
for an independent evaluation.

## What we want to learn

1. How many participants move into work, education or training, and how long they stay there.
2. Which parts of the programme (mentoring, placements, application support) make the most
   difference, and for whom.
3. What employers think of the placements, and whether they would take part again.

## What we need

- The evaluator must use mixed methods: an analysis of our monitoring data and qualitative
  research with participants, mentors and employers.
- The evaluator must interview at least 30 participants, including at least 10 who left the
  programme early.
- Anyone who meets participants under 18 must have an enhanced DBS check before fieldwork starts.
- The evaluator must follow our safeguarding policy and complete our safeguarding induction.
- The evaluator must work to a recognised research ethics framework and obtain informed
  consent from every interviewee.
- All personal data must be handled in line with UK GDPR, and the evaluator will sign our data
  sharing agreement before receiving any monitoring data.
- We would welcome a theory of change approach, but this is not essential.
- The evaluator should have experience of evaluating employment or youth programmes.

The total budget is up to £35,000 including VAT and expenses. Payment will be made in three

## How to apply

Proposals must not exceed 10 pages, plus CVs of the key staff. They should set out your
understanding of the brief, your proposed methods, your team and a breakdown of costs.

Submissions must be received no later than 5pm on 30 November 2026, by email to
evaluation@northvaleyouth.example. We expect to interview shortlisted applicants in the week
of 7 December 2026.

Proposals will be assessed on understanding of the brief (25%), methodology (40%), team and
experience (20%) and value for money (15%).
