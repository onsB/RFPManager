# Request for Proposals: Gull Point Beach Café and Kayak Rental Concession

City of Port Alder, Department of Parks and Recreation

The City invites proposals from qualified operators to run a seasonal beach café and kayak
rental concession from the City-owned building at Gull Point Beach.

## Key dates

- RFP release: October 26, 2026
- Mandatory pre-proposal site visit: November 10, 2026 at 10:00 AM PST
- Written questions due: November 17, 2026
- Proposals are due by 3:00 PM PST on Friday, December 11, 2026
- Anticipated City Council approval: February 2027

## The opportunity

Gull Point Beach receives about 400,000 visitors a year, most of them between Memorial Day and
Labor Day. The concession building (1,800 sq ft, with a kitchen and a rental shed) has been
closed since 2024. The City is offering a five-year concession agreement with one optional
five-year renewal.

## Operating requirements

- The concessionaire must operate the café and kayak rental daily from Memorial Day to Labor
  Day, at least from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and on weekends in May and September.
- All kayak rentals must include life jackets, and staff must hold current first aid and CPR
  certification.
- The concessionaire must coordinate with City lifeguards and must suspend kayak rentals
  whenever the lifeguards raise a red flag.
- No glass containers may be sold for use on the beach.
- The concessionaire may apply to the State for a beer and wine license; this is optional.

## Condition of the premises

The building is offered as-is. Before opening, the concessionaire must, at its own cost,
repair the roof, replace the grease trap and bring the kitchen up to County health code.
The City has not estimated the cost of these works. The concessionaire must obtain a County
health permit before opening, and any new signage or structure on the beach requires approval
from the State Coastal Commission. The City will maintain the parking lot and public restrooms.

## Financial terms

- The concessionaire will pay the greater of a minimum annual guarantee of $48,000 or 12% of
  gross receipts, paid monthly.
- A security deposit of $25,000 is due when the agreement is signed.
- Monthly gross receipts reports must be submitted by the 15th of the following month.
- The initial term of the agreement is three (3) years.

## Insurance

The concessionaire must carry commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000
per occurrence, naming the City as additional insured, and workers' compensation as required
by State law. The City reserves the right to require higher limits if alcohol is sold.

## Evaluation and selection

Proposals will be scored out of 100 points:

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Relevant experience operating food or recreation concessions | 30 |
| Business plan and financial offer | 35 |
| Plan and funding for facility improvements | 20 |
| Community benefit and local hiring | 15 |

The City reserves the right to reject any and all proposals, and to negotiate with the
next-ranked proposer if negotiations with the first fail.

## Proposal contents

Proposals must not exceed 15 pages and must be emailed as a single PDF to
concessions@portalder.example. Include financial statements for the last two years and a
description of at least one concession or restaurant you have operated.
