# Invitation to Tender: Tenant Self-Service Portal

Harbourside Homes, a housing association managing 4,200 homes across the Calder Bay area,
invites tenders for the design, build and support of a tenant self-service portal.

## Key dates

| Milestone | Date |
|---|---|
| ITT published | 2 November 2026 |
| Clarification questions deadline | 5pm on 20 November 2026 |
| Tender deadline | 12 noon on 4 December 2026 |
| Expected contract award | 18 December 2026 |
| Contract start | 11 January 2027 |

## Background

Our tenants currently report repairs by phone, and our contact centre handles around 9,000
calls a month. Around 70% of tenants have told us they would rather use a website or app.
Our board has set a target of handling half of all repair reports online by the end of 2028.

## Scope of work

The supplier must deliver a web portal, usable on mobile phones, that lets tenants:

- report a repair, with photos, and track its progress;
- view their rent balance and payment history;
- book, change or cancel a repair appointment;
- update their contact details.

The portal must integrate with our housing management system, Keystone HMS (version 4),
through its REST API. The supplier must migrate the 3,100 existing online accounts from our
current system without asking tenants to register again.

The supplier should offer SMS notifications for appointment reminders. Suppliers may also
propose a native app, priced separately as an option.

## Accessibility, security and data

- The portal must meet WCAG 2.2 level AA, tested with assistive technology users before go-live.
- The supplier must hold Cyber Essentials Plus certification at the time of tender.
- An independent penetration test must be completed before go-live, and the supplier must fix
  all critical and high findings at its own cost.
- All tenant data must be stored and processed in the UK, in line with UK GDPR.
- The service should be available 99.5% of the time, measured monthly.

## Budget and contract

The maximum budget is £180,000 excluding VAT over the whole contract, including
implementation, hosting and support. Implementation must be priced as a fixed price.

The contract will commence on 4 January 2027 and run for three years, with an option to
extend by up to 12 months. The portal must be live for all tenants by 31 March 2027.

Harbourside Homes reserves the right not to award a contract, and to cancel this procurement
at any time without liability. Harbourside will provide a test environment for Keystone HMS
during the implementation.

## Evaluation criteria

Tenders will be scored on quality (60%) and price (40%). Quality is broken down as follows:

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Delivery approach and plan | 25% |
| Accessibility and user experience | 15% |
| Security and data protection | 10% |
| Social value | 10% |

Tenders must be submitted through the Calder Bay procurement portal. Your response must not

name the key people who will work on the project. Applications from consortia are welcome.
Please include two references from similar projects completed in the last three years.
