# Invitation to Tender: Volunteer Coordination Platform

Issued by Fenmoor Community Trust. This is a synthetic test document written for the
evaluation harness; the organisation and all details are fictional.

## 1. Background

Fenmoor Community Trust supports around 40 local charities. We aim to double the number of active volunteers in the borough by 2030 and to make volunteering accessible to people of all ages. Today, charities coordinate volunteers with spreadsheets and email, and many shifts go unfilled.

## 2. Scope of work

### 2.1 Platform

The supplier must deliver a web platform where charities can post volunteering opportunities and volunteers can sign up for shifts.

The platform must meet WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility standards.

The platform should send reminder emails to volunteers 24 hours before each shift.

Pages should load in under 2 seconds on a standard 4G connection.

A native mobile app is not required.

The supplier may also propose an SMS notification feature as a separately priced option.

### 2.2 Training and handover

The supplier will need to provide:

- two online training sessions for charity staff
- a written user guide for volunteers
- three months of post-launch support

## 3. Data protection and compliance

The supplier must comply with UK GDPR. A data processing agreement must be signed before any personal data is shared.

All personal data must be stored in the UK.

The supplier must not use volunteers' personal data for marketing or to train AI models.

The supplier must hold professional indemnity insurance of at least £1 million.

ISO 27001 certification is desirable but not essential.

## 4. Budget and timeline

Bids must not exceed £60,000 excluding VAT, including all licences and support for the first year.

The supplier must meet the following milestones:

| Milestone | Date |
|---|---|
| Platform live for five pilot charities | 31 March 2027 |
| Full launch to all member charities | 30 June 2027 |

## 5. How to respond

Proposals must be submitted through the Trust's procurement portal by 5pm on 15 January 2027.

Proposals should not exceed 10 pages.

Each proposal should include:

- a delivery plan with milestones
- a fixed price breakdown
- two references from similar projects

Individual CVs are not required. Applications from consortia are welcome.

We expect to announce the outcome in February 2027.
